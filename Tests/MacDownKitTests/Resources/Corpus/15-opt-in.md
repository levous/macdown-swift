# Opt-in syntax

Highlight: ==marked text== and ==two words== but not = single =.

Superscript: x^2, 10^(-6), e^(i pi) and a lone ^ caret.

Autolink: https://example.org/page, www.example.org and hello@example.org.

Smart punctuation: "double quotes," 'single quotes,' it's, en -- dash, em --- dash, ellipsis...

Inside code nothing changes: `"quotes" -- ==mark== x^2 https://example.org`.

Quote markup that MacDown used to render as <q>: "a quotation".
