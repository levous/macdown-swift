# Inline formatting

Emphasis: *single asterisks* and _single underscores_.
Strong: **double asterisks** and __double underscores__.
Both: ***three asterisks*** and ___three underscores___.
Nested: **strong with *emphasis* inside** and *emphasis with **strong** inside*.

Intra-word: un*frigging*believable, snake_case_name, foo__bar__baz.

Unbalanced: *not closed, **also not closed, and a lone * star.

Escapes: \*not emphasis\*, \_not emphasis\_, \`not code\`, \# not a header, 1\. not a list, \[not a link\].

Code spans: `plain`, `` code with ` backtick ``, ` padded `, `*not emphasis*` and `<b>not html</b>`.

Strikethrough: ~~struck~~ and ~single tilde~.

Line break with two spaces  
and with a backslash\
and a plain soft break
in one paragraph.

Entities: &copy; &amp; &lt; &#169; &#x2603; and a bare & ampersand.
