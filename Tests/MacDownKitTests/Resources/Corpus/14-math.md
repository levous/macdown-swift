# Math

Display math with dollars:

$$
\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3}
$$

Display math with brackets: \\[ a_1 + a_2 = b_{12} \\]

Inline with parentheses: \\( e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0 \\)

Inline with dollars (needs its setting): $A^T_S = B$ and $x_1 * y_2$.

Underscores and stars inside math must survive: $a_b * c_d$ and $$x_{*} = y_{*}$$.

Currency is not math: it costs $5 and $10, or $1600.

Escaped dollars: \$5 and \$x\$ stay literal.

Math-looking text in code: `$x_1$` and

```
$$ not math in a fence $$
```

Unclosed: $no closing dollar.
